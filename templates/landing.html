For county health teams & NGOs

See outbreaks coming, days earlier

Anyone can report illness at home from the Tujali menu. Reports are grouped into warning signs like fever, diarrhoea or rash with fever, and mapped by county. Unusual rises are flagged automatically, so teams can act before an outbreak spreads.
